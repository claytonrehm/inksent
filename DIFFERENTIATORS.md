# Inksent — Why We Win, and Where This Is Going

Two parts: (1) what to *say today* to title companies and notaries — the things that make us obviously better than a phone-tree signing service; and (2) a forward-looking, AI-native vision so we lead this field instead of catching up to it.

---

## 1. Why title companies / escrow / lenders choose us

> *"Are you ready for the future of notary assignment? Try us once."*

- **~30-minute confirmation, not phone tag.** We broadcast each order to *every* covering, vetted agent at once. First to accept wins. You're not waiting on one person to call back.
- **Watch it happen, live.** A real-time tracker shows *Order received → Agent confirmed → On the way → Arrived → Complete.* You're never wondering "is this actually happening?"
- **Only agents who can handle real closings.** Every agent is NNA-certified, background-checked, E&O-insured, and **vetted for purchase/refi experience** — and the system *blocks* dispatch to anyone whose credentials have lapsed. No rookies at your closing table.
- **Documents are airtight.** Send an updated package and the old one is *deleted* + the agent is re-notified — nobody ever signs a stale set. Docs are encrypted, access-controlled, and **auto-purged after 14 days** (GLBA/CCPA-aligned), so your borrowers' data isn't sitting around forever.
- **It just runs.** Auto-dispatch, auto-tracking, auto-invoicing, auto-payment recovery. If something slips (no-show, failed payout, missed payment) the system catches and self-heals it — and a human is alerted *before* it becomes your problem.
- **Pay your way.** Per-signing now; consolidated monthly net-30 invoicing or ACH when you want it — all automated.
- **Transparent flat pricing.** No surprise line items.

## 3. The leading edge — where we take this (AI-native vision)

The signing industry still runs on phone calls, spreadsheets, and faxed instructions. As AI matures, the winner will be the platform that's **AI-native from the dispatch layer up**. Here's the roadmap that keeps us in front:

### Near-term (months) — sharpen the wedge
- **AI document pre-check.** Before the agent leaves, AI scans the loan package for missing signature/initial tabs, dates, and notary blocks → flags gaps to title *before* the appointment. Fewer failed signings = fewer redraws = our #1 selling point.
- **AI scan-back QC.** After signing, AI verifies the scan-backs — every signature, initial, and stamp present — and flags problems *at the table* while the agent can still fix them. Near-zero kickbacks.
- **Predictive dispatch.** Match on *who's most likely to accept and complete flawlessly*, not just who's nearest — learned from acceptance speed, ratings, completion history, time of day.
- **24/7 AI intake + status line.** A voice/chat agent places orders and answers "where's my notary?" around the clock — true zero-touch ops, so the business runs without you on the phone.

### Mid-term (1–2 yrs) — change the product shape
- **RON & hybrid signings.** Remote Online Notarization is the biggest structural shift in this field. Offer in-person, **RON**, or hybrid from one order form — be the platform that does all three as more states authorize it.
- **Identity & fraud defense.** AI-assisted ID verification at the table (scan ID → liveness + validation). Real-estate wire/identity fraud is exploding; "we verify the signer" becomes a headline feature for lenders.
- **System integrations.** Push/pull orders directly from title production systems (Qualia, ResWare, SoftPro). Once we're in their workflow, we're sticky.
- **Borrower concierge.** Automated, friendly prep (what to bring, ID rules) + an AI assistant answering borrower questions — smoother signings, better reviews.

### Long-term (the moat) — own the rails
- **Dynamic pricing.** Price by urgency, distance, complexity, and live supply — protect margin on rural/last-minute work while staying competitive on routine jobs.
- **Predictive coverage & recruiting.** Forecast demand by area/time and recruit agents *before* you have gaps — turn coverage from reactive to strategic.
- **Verifiable agent reputation.** A portable, tamper-proof record of an agent's credentials + signing history — trust that travels with them and that lenders can rely on.
- **Data products.** Anonymized, aggregated turn-time and market insight back to title partners — we become their benchmark, not just their vendor.

**The thesis:** every step a human does today in a signing — match, prep, verify, QC, pay, reconcile — becomes an AI-assisted step that's faster, cheaper, and more reliable. Build that platform now, while competitors are still dialing notaries one at a time.
