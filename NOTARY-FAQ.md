# Notary Recruiting — Quick Answers

Copy-paste replies for common questions from notary applicants. Keep the tone warm
and **honest** — never overpromise job volume. Accurate to how the system works today.

---

## 💵 Pay

**"How are signings paid?"**
> You're paid **$90 per completed signing**, sent by **secure direct deposit** straight to your bank — automatically, once the title company pays for that signing. No invoicing or chasing checks. When you're approved, you do a quick 2-minute bank connection through Stripe (our payments partner), and payouts just land after each job. 😊

**"How much do I make per signing?"**
> $90 per completed signing here in San Diego. As we grow we may pay more for complex or longer-distance jobs.

**"How fast do I get paid?"**
> Payout is released automatically once the client pays for that signing — no waiting on you to invoice or follow up. It deposits straight to your bank.

**"Do I get a 1099 / how do taxes work?"**
> You're a 1099 independent contractor. Stripe handles the tax form automatically, and you'll get a 1099 if you earn $600+ in a year.

## 📲 Getting jobs

**"How do I get assigned signings?"**
> When a signing opens in your area, you get it by **text and email**. First to accept gets the job — no phone tag. You can accept or pass on anything; there's no obligation.

**"How many jobs will I get / how much work is there?"**
> Full transparency: we're a new, growing service actively signing up title and escrow partners, so volume builds over the coming weeks — it won't be a flood on day one. Getting in early means you're first in the rotation as orders start flowing. *(Don't promise specific numbers.)*

**"Do I have to accept every job?"**
> Not at all — you choose what you accept, set your own schedule, and there are no minimums. Decline anything that doesn't work for you.

## ✅ Requirements

**"What do I need to join?"**
> A valid notary commission, NNA certification, current E&O insurance, a recent background check, and a dual-tray laser printer. You'll confirm these during onboarding after you're approved.

**"Do I need NNA certification / E&O insurance?"**
> Yes — we work with NNA-certified signing agents who carry their own E&O insurance. You'll enter those details when you onboard.

**"What areas will I cover / how far do I travel?"**
> You set your home ZIP and how far you're willing to travel — we only send you jobs inside your range.

**"Do you need bilingual agents?"**
> Yes! If you speak another language fluently, note it — it means more jobs matched to you.

## 🤝 The relationship

**"Am I an employee or a contractor?"**
> You're an independent contractor — you set your own hours, use your own equipment, work for whoever you like (non-exclusive), and there's no cost or commitment to join.

**"Is there a fee to join?"**
> None. Free to apply, free to join, no minimums, no exclusivity.
