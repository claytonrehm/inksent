# Inksent — Roadmap & Growth Ideas

Future bets, parked until the core (signing dispatch in SD) is humming. Not to be
built now — captured so they aren't lost.

---

## Core model (today)
Charge title companies **$175**/signing, pay notaries **$90**, keep the **$85** spread.
Grow by adding notaries (supply) and title companies (demand). The demand side is
always the constraint — that's where sales effort pays off most.

---

## Growth levers (in rough priority)

### 1. 🔥 RON — Remote Online Notarization
Notarizations done over video. **Higher margin, no travel, scales infinitely** (no
coverage limits). Where the industry is heading. Add once core is steady — partner
with a RON platform or recruit RON-capable notaries.

### 2. 🔥 White-label the software (SaaS)
The dispatch engine (radius matching, backup-on-cancel, document routing,
auto-payouts, live tracking) is genuinely strong. License it to *other* signing
services as SaaS — sell the picks and shovels, not just the service. Highest ceiling,
bigger pivot.

### 3. Geographic expansion
Same model, more metros/states. The main volume lever once SD is proven. Run ads to
recruit notaries in each new market.

### 4. Premium / ancillary services
- **Rush / same-day** surcharge
- **Apostille services** (international doc authentication — higher fee, less competition)
- **Specialty signings** (structured settlements, etc.)

### 5. Notary training / courses
Once there's a brand, sell a "get more signings" course/certification to notaries.
Proven money-maker (the Loan Signing System model). Adjacent business, captive audience.

### 6. LO synergy (quiet edge)
Founder is an active loan officer. Title-company relationships built via Inksent feed
the personal loan pipeline, and vice versa. Cross-pollinate the two businesses.

---

## Scaling the team (when volume justifies)
- **Ads** → recruit notaries at scale (cheap, scalable supply)
- **Sales reps** → call & close title companies (relationship-driven demand)
- **Coordinator / support hire** → only once order volume outgrows the automation

---

## Deferred product ideas
- **Mobile app** — NOT needed for a long time. Notaries get jobs by text + accept via
  link (no install = a feature). Build only at real scale if notaries want a dashboard.
- **Client quick-order / saved accounts** — build when the first repeat client appears,
  designed around how *they* order. Foundation (`/portal`) already exists.
- **Title-software integrations** (Qualia, ResWare, SoftPro order APIs) — for a single
  high-volume "whale" account that justifies it. Makes you very sticky.
- **Email-to-order** — let trusted clients forward an email that becomes an order.
- **Real-time escalation (sub-daily cron)** — needs Vercel Pro; today's cron is daily.

---

## Don'ts (intentional)
- **Don't charge notaries to join** — keep supply free; make money on the spread.
- **Don't add fake reviews** — illegal (FTC) + B2B buyers spot them. Use founder
  credibility now; collect real testimonials after the first signings.
- **Don't build the mobile app early** — text-based flow already wins on friction.
