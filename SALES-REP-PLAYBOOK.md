# Inksent — Sales Rep Playbook

Everything you need to start selling Inksent to title & escrow companies in San Diego.
Read the one-page sell sheet, then use the call script and email templates below.

> **Hard rule: never overpromise.** Only say what's on this sheet. If you're unsure
> whether we can do something, say "let me confirm and get right back to you" — never
> guess. A promise we can't keep burns a title relationship for good.

---

## 1. One-Page Sell Sheet

**What Inksent is:** A notary signing assignment & tracking platform for title, escrow,
and lenders. We dispatch vetted signing agents, track every closing live, and handle
invoicing and notary payment automatically. **inksent.co**

**The hook — there's no cost to try us:**
No subscription. No setup fee. No contract. The title company only pays per signing they
actually send us. "Try us once" is the entire ask.

**Why title companies choose us:**

- **~30-minute confirmation, not phone tag.** We broadcast each order to *every* covering,
  vetted agent at once — first to accept wins. No waiting on one notary to call back.
- **Watch it happen, live.** Real-time tracker: Order received → Agent confirmed → On the
  way → Arrived → Complete. You're never wondering if it's actually happening.
- **Only agents who can handle real closings.** Every agent is NNA-certified,
  background-checked, E&O-insured, and vetted for purchase/refi experience. The system
  blocks dispatch to anyone whose credentials have lapsed. No rookies at the closing table.
- **Documents are airtight.** Send an updated package and the old one is deleted + the agent
  re-notified — nobody signs a stale set. Docs are encrypted, access-controlled, and
  auto-purged after 14 days (GLBA/CCPA-aligned).
- **It just runs.** Auto-dispatch, auto-tracking, auto-invoicing, auto-payment recovery. If
  something slips (no-show, missed payment), the system catches it and a human is alerted
  before it becomes your problem.
- **Pay your way.** Per-signing now, or consolidated monthly net-30 invoicing when you want.
- **Transparent flat pricing.** No surprise line items.

**Coverage:** San Diego County [confirm exact radius/cities with Clayton].
**Pricing:** [Clayton to confirm what you disclose on a cold call — e.g. flat $/signing.]

**Your one-sentence pitch:**
> "We're a San Diego notary signing platform — vetted agents, ~30-minute confirmation, and a
> live tracker on every closing — and there's no cost to try us, you only pay per signing you

## 2. Cold-Call Script

**Opener (earn 20 seconds):**
> "Hi [Name], this is [You] with Inksent — I'll be quick. I know I'm calling out of the
> blue. Do you have 20 seconds, or did I catch you mid-fire-drill?"

*(If busy: "Totally get it — when's a better time, later today or tomorrow morning?")*

**The hook:**
> "We're a notary signing platform here in San Diego. The reason I'm calling — most signing
> services have you call around and wait for one notary to call back. We broadcast every
> order to all our vetted agents at once, so you usually get a confirmation in about 30
> minutes, and you can watch the whole closing live — on the way, arrived, complete. And
> there's no cost to set up or try it — you only pay per signing you actually send us."

**Discovery (get them talking):**
- "How are you handling your signings right now — in-house list, a service, or both?"
- "What's the most annoying part — getting confirmations, last-minute scrambles, redraws?"
- "How often does a signing fall through or come back with missing signatures?"

*(Listen for the pain. Then connect it back: "That's exactly what we fixed with X.")*

**The close — go for the demo, not the sale:**
> "I don't want to sell you anything on this call. Let me show you a quick branded demo
> built for [Their Company] — takes 5 minutes. Are you better mornings or afternoons this
> week?"

*(If they won't book: "No problem — what's the best email? I'll send you a personalized demo
link you can click through whenever, and I'll follow up Thursday.")*

### Objection handling

- **"We already have a signing service."**
  > "Most of the title companies we work with did too — they keep us as a backup for when
  > their main service can't cover or can't confirm fast enough. There's no cost to have us
  > on standby. Worst case, you've got a faster option in your back pocket for the next
  > scramble. Want me to set that up?"

- **"Not interested / no time."**
  > "Fair enough. Can I just send you a 5-minute demo link? If it's not for you, delete it —
  > no follow-up calls. What's the best email?"

- **"Just send me some information."**
  > "Happy to — and I'll make it specific to you, not a generic brochure. What's the email,
  > and are you the right person for signing decisions or is that someone else there?"
  > *(Always get the decision-maker's name.)*

- **"How much is it?"**
  > "[Clayton's pricing line.] And remember there's no setup or subscription — you only pay
  > when you send us a signing, so trying us costs you nothing."

- **"Are your notaries any good?"**
  > "Every one is NNA-certified, background-checked, E&O-insured, and vetted for purchase and
  > refi experience — and our system literally blocks us from sending anyone whose
  > credentials have lapsed. No rookies at your closing table."

## 5. Daily Rhythm (suggested)

- **Block 2–3 hours of calls** when title offices are staffed (mid-morning, early afternoon).
- **Send the branded demo link** to everyone who'll give you an email — it's your best tool.
- **Goal isn't the sale on call one** — it's the demo, then the first test signing.
- **The first test signing is the real win.** Once a title company sends one and it goes
  smoothly, they come back. Your job is to get them to that first one.
